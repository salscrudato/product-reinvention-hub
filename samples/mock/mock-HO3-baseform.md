# HOMEOWNERS 3 – SPECIAL FORM — HO 00 03 05 11

## AGREEMENT
We provide the insurance described in this policy in return for the premium and
compliance with all applicable provisions.

## SECTION I — PROPERTY COVERAGES

**Coverage A — Dwelling.** We cover the dwelling on the residence premises against
risk of direct physical loss. This coverage is mandatory and is rated.

**Coverage B — Other Structures.** We cover other structures on the residence
premises separated from the dwelling. The limit is 10% of Coverage A; it may be
increased by endorsement HO 04 48.

**Coverage C — Personal Property.** We cover personal property owned or used by an
insured. The Coverage C limit is a percentage of Coverage A (default 50%). Loss is
settled at actual cash value unless the Personal Property Replacement Cost Loss
Settlement endorsement HO 04 90 is attached.

**Coverage D — Loss of Use.** We cover the additional living expense when a covered
loss makes the residence uninhabitable. The limit is 30% of Coverage A.

## SECTION I — CONDITIONS AND OPTIONS

Water that backs up through sewers or drains is excluded under this base form unless
the Water Back-Up and Sump Discharge or Overflow endorsement HO 04 95 is attached.

High-value articles (jewelry, furs, fine arts) may be scheduled at agreed value by
the Scheduled Personal Property Endorsement HO 04 61.

## SECTION II — LIABILITY COVERAGES

**Coverage E — Personal Liability.** We pay damages an insured is legally liable for
because of bodily injury or property damage. Coverage E is optional above the default
limit and is rated.

**Coverage F — Medical Payments to Others.** We pay necessary medical expenses for
persons injured on the insured location. If the Coverage F limit of $5,000 is
selected, Coverage E must be at least $300,000.

## ELIGIBILITY

This policy is available only for an owner-occupied one- to four-family dwelling used
for residential purposes. A seasonal or secondary dwelling is ineligible unless a
companion primary policy is in force.

## RATING BASIS (informational)

Premium is developed from the Coverage A amount, the rating territory, the protection
class and the construction type. A minimum policy premium of $500 applies. Detailed
rates are published in the separate Homeowners Rate Manual.
