# COMMERCIAL GENERAL LIABILITY COVERAGE FORM — CG 00 01 04 13

A mock base coverage form for exercising grounded extraction on the GL line (upload
this in a GL product's Coverages tab → "Extract"). This form deliberately contains no
rates, so the rating section should come back empty with an explicit note.

## SECTION I — COVERAGES

**Coverage A — Bodily Injury and Property Damage Liability.** We pay those sums the
insured becomes legally obligated to pay as damages because of bodily injury or
property damage to which this insurance applies. Coverage A is mandatory and is rated.

**Coverage B — Personal and Advertising Injury Liability.** We pay those sums the
insured becomes legally obligated to pay as damages because of personal and
advertising injury. Coverage B is mandatory and is rated.

**Coverage C — Medical Payments.** We pay medical expenses for bodily injury caused by
an accident on premises the insured owns or rents. Coverage C is optional.

## EXCLUSIONS AND OPTIONS

Liquor liability is excluded under this base form. Where the insured is in the business
of selling or serving alcoholic beverages, coverage may be added by the Amendment of
Liquor Liability Exclusion endorsement CG 21 50.

Employee benefits liability is not covered under this base form; it may be added by the
Employee Benefits Liability Coverage endorsement CG 04 35.

## ELIGIBILITY

This coverage form applies to a commercial risk with operations described in the
Declarations. A risk with no described premises or operations is ineligible.

## PREMIUM

The premiums for this Coverage Part are computed in accordance with our rules, rates,
rating plans, premiums and minimum premiums, which are published in a separate
Commercial Lines rate manual and are not stated in this form.
