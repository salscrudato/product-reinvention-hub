Operating principles

Carried from the IH and UX v1 packs, with one addition: templates are contracts.

Templates are contracts

The three committed workbooks define the export targets. Structure is read from the files at build and test time - sheet names, header rows, column order - never re-typed. Goldens diff against them.

Tests and lint are law

A rule you do not lint is a rule you do not have. Every closed item ships a permanent test or lint gate.

Protect the identity

Productization, not redesign. The eight protect-list invariants bind agent and judge alike.

Conservation on the way out

Flagged-not-dropped applies to exports exactly as to imports: every canonical entity is mapped or listed on an UNMAPPED appendix - never silently omitted.

One writer, escalate on evidence

Subagents investigate and judge; only the main agent edits. Parked items unlock on operator GO or a proven trigger.
