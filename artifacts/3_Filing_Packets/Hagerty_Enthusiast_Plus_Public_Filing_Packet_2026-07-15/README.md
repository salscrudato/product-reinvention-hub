# Hagerty Enthusiast+ public filing research packet

**As of:** 2026-07-15

> **Important:** This archive is a verified public-source research and retrieval package, **not a complete official SERFF attachment export**.

## Bottom line

- The legal underwriting company identified for Enthusiast+ is **Drivers Edge Insurance Company (NAIC 23795)**, formerly Consolidated National Insurance Company.
- **Colorado is the only currently verified active state.** Hagerty's 2025 Form 10-K says the product launched there in Q3 2025, and the CFO stated on May 6, 2026 that E-Plus was in one state, Colorado.
- **Wisconsin is unresolved.** A September 10, 2025 trade report described it as the second launch, but the later May 6, 2026 issuer statement conflicts with that report.
- No other jurisdiction was verified as a live Enthusiast+ market in the reviewed sources through 2026-07-15. That is **not proof that no filing exists**.
- Drivers Edge reported certificates of authority in 42 states as of March 31, 2026. A certificate of authority is not evidence that Enthusiast+ has been approved, launched or is in force in a specific state.

## Why the official rate/rule/form files are missing

The public SERFF index exposed candidate internal filing-page IDs, but direct attachment access required a valid state SERFF session or returned an access/session error. The research did not bypass those controls. Consequently, this packet does **not** contain an official filing ZIP, rate manual, rule manual, actuarial memorandum, form schedule, regulator correspondence or disposition record for Enthusiast+.

## Correct interpretation

Use the workbook's classifications exactly as written:

- **Verified active / pilot** — direct issuer evidence supports a live state.
- **Reported rollout / unresolved** — a credible report exists, but later evidence conflicts or official filing status is unconfirmed.
- **No verified rollout found** — no reviewed public source established a live product; this does not establish absence.
