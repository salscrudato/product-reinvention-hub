# Research findings

## Verified carrier and product

Hagerty's 2025 Form 10-K states that it acquired Consolidated National Insurance Company in September 2024, renamed it Drivers Edge Insurance Company, launched Enthusiast+ in Colorado in Q3 2025, and planned a phased nationwide rollout over four years.

Primary source: https://s28.q4cdn.com/107308082/files/doc_financials/2025/ar/4b3894e9-e517-492e-a004-ab01967e5a6d.pdf

The Nebraska Department of Insurance company summary lists Drivers Edge Insurance Company under NAIC ID 23795.

Source: https://www.nebraska.gov/insurance/appointments/search/summary.cgi?naic=23795&rYear=2024&type=prop

## Latest verified state count

On Hagerty's May 6, 2026 earnings call, CFO Patrick McClymont said E-Plus was in one state, Colorado, and that the company was learning there before rolling out over time.

Transcript source: https://www.roic.ai/quote/HGTY/transcripts/2026-year/1-quarter
Official event confirmation: https://investor.hagerty.com/events-and-presentations/
Official 8-K confirming the call: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1840776/000184077626000019/hgty-20260506.htm

## Wisconsin conflict

Coverager reported on September 10, 2025 that Wisconsin was the second state launch and described product features including vehicles under construction, motorsports, off-road use, custom features, cherished salvage, spare parts, stated value, Motorsports Advantage, Overlander and Enjoy the Ride.

Source: https://coverager.com/hagerty-expands-drivers-edge-enthusiast-program-to-wisconsin/

Because the later issuer statement said the product was in one state, Colorado, Wisconsin is classified as reported/unresolved rather than verified active.

## Licensing is not rollout evidence

Hagerty's Q1 2026 Form 10-Q says Drivers Edge held certificates of authority in 42 states as of March 31, 2026. That establishes regulatory licensing breadth, not state-level Enthusiast+ approval or availability.

Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1840776/000184077626000021/hgty-20260331.htm

## Filing-access result

SERFF Filing Access is the official public-filing route where a participating state has made a filing public. Colorado and Wisconsin provide state access routes, but official attachment exports were not retrieved in this research environment because direct deep links required a valid state session or returned an access/session error.

SERFF information: https://www.serff.com/serff_filing_access.htm
Colorado access: https://filingaccess.serff.com/sfa/home/CO
Wisconsin guidance: https://oci.wi.gov/Pages/Companies/PolicyFormsRateFilingSearch.aspx
