# Limitations and completion path

## What is complete

The carrier identity, NAIC number, Colorado launch, planned phased rollout, later one-state statement, Wisconsin reporting conflict, public-access routes and inspected E-Docs exclusions are documented with source URLs.

## What is not complete

A complete product packet requires the official regulator-maintained attachment export for every operative filing and supplement. None of the following was retrieved from SERFF for Drivers Edge Enthusiast+:

1. Filing summary and transmittal.
2. Form schedule and filed forms.
3. Rate and rule manuals.
4. Rating factors, tables or algorithms made public.
5. Actuarial memorandum and exhibits.
6. Supporting documents.
7. Objections, responses and correspondence.
8. Disposition, approval, withdrawal and effective-date records.
9. Amendments, supplements and superseded filing history.

## Why

SERFF Filing Access is state-specific. Candidate filing-summary deep links required an active state session or returned an access/session error. This research did not attempt to circumvent those controls and did not assign the candidate IDs to Colorado or Wisconsin without evidence.

## Completion sequence

1. Submit the included Colorado and Wisconsin public-records requests.
2. From each state's SFA entry page, search the legal carrier name **Drivers Edge Insurance Company**, NAIC **23795**, former name **Consolidated National Insurance Company**, and product variants **Enthusiast+**, **Enthusiast Plus**, **E-Plus**, **Drivers Edge**, **modern enthusiast**, **personal auto** and **private passenger auto**.
3. Search filing types Form, Rate, Rule and Combination for January 1, 2024 through 2026-07-15.
4. Download the full attachment export for every responsive base filing, amendment, supplement and replacement filing.
5. Build a version chain using submission, disposition, implementation and effective dates. Do not assume the newest submission is the current operative product.
6. Reconcile every form number and edition date against the form schedule, every rate/rule attachment against the disposition, and every supplement against the superseded version.
7. Obtain written regulator confirmation of the complete current-in-force set, especially for Wisconsin because the public reporting conflicts with the later issuer statement.
8. Replace the workbook's retrieval fields from **No / Not retrieved** to the verified status and add the regulator-provided files to state-specific folders.

## Evidence standard

A state should be labeled active only when at least one of these is available:

- an official filing disposition and effective date supporting current use;
- written regulator confirmation;
- a direct current issuer statement identifying the state as live; or
- an official quote/application flow that clearly identifies the underwriting company and current state availability, corroborated by filed forms.

Licensing, certificates of authority, appointment records, marketing pages and feature one-pagers are not sufficient on their own.
