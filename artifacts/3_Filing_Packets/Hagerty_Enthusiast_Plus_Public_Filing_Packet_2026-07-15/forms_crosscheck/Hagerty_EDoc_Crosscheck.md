# Hagerty E-Docs crosscheck

The public Hagerty E-Docs index was reviewed as a possible mirror of the requested Enthusiast+ forms. It cannot be used as a substitute for the Drivers Edge SERFF filing packet.

## Result

The inspected current-looking Colorado and feature-related forms were labeled **Essentia Insurance Company**, not **Drivers Edge Insurance Company**. They are therefore excluded from the requested filing set. Feature-name overlap such as Motorsports Advantage is a research lead only and does not establish carrier or product identity.

## Inspected leads

- **AC 001 CO 09 25 - Colorado State Endorsement** (Colorado): carrier observed `Essentia Insurance Company`; classification `Exclude from Enthusiast+ packet`. Public form is labeled Essentia, not Drivers Edge. Source: https://www.hagerty.com/EDocs/Download?id=5142504
- **AC 002 CO 02 26 - Part C - Colorado** (Colorado): carrier observed `Essentia Insurance Company`; classification `Exclude from Enthusiast+ packet`. Public form is labeled Essentia, not Drivers Edge. Source: https://www.hagerty.com/EDocs/Download?id=5142904
- **AC 115 11 22 - Motorsports Advantage** (Multi-state; Colorado listed): carrier observed `Essentia Insurance Company`; classification `Feature-name lead only`. The feature name overlaps public reporting, but the form is an Essentia form and cannot be assigned to Drivers Edge Enthusiast+. Source: https://www.hagerty.com/EDocs/Download?id=5141546
- **AC 127 01 24 - Motorsports, Autocross and HPDE** (Multi-state; Colorado listed): carrier observed `Essentia Insurance Company`; classification `Feature-name lead only`. The public form is an Essentia endorsement, not verified as part of the Drivers Edge product. Source: https://www.hagerty.com/EDocs/Download?id=5141977
- **Index - Hagerty E-Docs state indexes** (Colorado and Wisconsin indexes): carrier observed `Mixed carriers / programs`; classification `Index crosscheck only`. No Drivers Edge label or unambiguous Enthusiast+ form set was found in the inspected public index entries. Source: https://www.hagerty.com/edocs

## Rule for packet inclusion

A form should enter the Enthusiast+ packet only when the filing record, form itself, form schedule or regulator confirmation ties it to Drivers Edge Insurance Company and the operative Enthusiast+ program.
