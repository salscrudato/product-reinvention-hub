Hagerty Product Hub Presentation Brief
Purpose: Content spec for an interactive presentation showcasing the Product Reinvention Hub and why it matters to Hagerty.
Core Narrative (Kurt's framing)
Thesis: This work must be done regardless of who executes it. The Hub is how Hagerty gets it done faster, cheaper, and with less internal lift. [FW: Hagert...tion Track | Outlook]
Audience posture: Hagerty agrees the scope is necessary and is willing to invest. Their concern is timeline vs. people commitment. The presentation must resolve that tension, not restate the scope. [FW: Hagert...tion Track | Outlook]
Five Framing Pillars
1. Strategy Before Rollout

Lead with product effectiveness, feedback loops, scalability.
Colorado is an input, not the strategy. Do not use "market fit" language.
Strategy clarity is the prerequisite to rationalization and rollout sequencing.

2. Core vs. E+ as Two-Product, 50-State Design

Reject the "E+ is slightly different from Core" framing.
Present as intentional rationalization: shared components plus deliberate deltas.
Payoff: reuse accelerates rollout, reduces dual maintenance, sustains governance.

3. Full Product Development Lifecycle

Expand the picture upstream (research, design) and downstream (monitoring, feedback).
Most delays originate upstream, not inside Duck Creek.
Governance must cover filing and non-filing changes.

4. Methodology First, Tooling Second

Tooling is illustrative, not prescriptive.
Slides target ~15 to 20 minutes; the rest is methodology and Hub demo.
Avoid any perception of selling a tool. Sell the approach.

5. Resolve the Timeline and Staffing Objection

Show sample deliverables at target depth (PCS is the reference case).
Make Hagerty resource and decision assumptions explicit so they go straight into SOW.
Distinguish must define now vs. can refine post-market.
E+ is the hard part: approach it as documenting current core, then decisioning rate, form, rule deviations. [FW: Hagert...tion Track | Outlook]

Why the Hub Matters to Hagerty (the "so what")

Speed to market: feedback loop plus rationalization compresses filing and rollout cycles.
Scalability beyond Colorado: a repeatable model for 50-state, two-product delivery.
Reduced dual maintenance: intentional reuse across Core and E+.
Governance that unblocks IT: upstream decisions arrive filing-ready, cutting Duck Creek rework.
Lower internal lift: the Hub does the heavy lifting Hagerty was concerned about staffing. [FW: Hagert...tion Track | Outlook]

Suggested Interactive Structure

Opening frame: "The work is necessary. Here is how the Hub makes it feasible."
Pillar walkthrough: five interactive sections, one per pillar above.
Live Hub demo surface: methodology-first, tooling as illustration.
Sample deliverable deep-dive: PCS reference artifact.
Commitment matrix: what Hagerty owns, what Accenture owns, what goes into SOW.
Now vs. later: what must be defined pre-launch vs. tunable in-market.

Design language: Apple-inspired restraint, light default with dark toggle, one accent color per surface, generous whitespace, subtle glassmorphism, 200 to 400ms spring motion.
