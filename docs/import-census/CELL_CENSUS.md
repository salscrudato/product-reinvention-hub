## Findings (baseline, 2026-07-16, corpus = 4 hagerty fixtures + 6 reference workbooks)

Evidence source: the table above plus docs/import-census/BASELINE/*.census.json
(full per-sheet detail). ASCII only; counts are from the census's RAW-substance
basis (any cell with a non-empty raw verbatim, whitespace kept, merge shadows
counted then accounted).

### F-C1. Conservation holds everywhere

10/10 files censused with ZERO crashes, ZERO timeouts, and the accounting
identity EXACT on every sheet (entries sum to nonEmpty; shadows auto-accounted).
The rollup denominator math (substanceCoverage) is live and unit-locked.

### F-C2. PCM Coverages version-copy trio: CONFIRMED (the live near-dup case)

Product_Component_Model_Coverages.xlsx clusters exactly as the pack predicted:
a header-basis cluster of the "Hacked"/"Main" copies (13,477 / 13,474 raw cells)
plus a name-basis cluster pairing the 2,724-cell copy with the 13,477 original.
The pack's quoted sizes (13,463 / 13,460 / 2,710) differ by ~14 cells - that is
the counting-basis delta (this census counts whitespace-only and shadow cells as
raw substance; the older sheet census did not). Cluster REPORTING only - the
extract-all + fold + conflict-to-review policy is CE3's.

### F-C3. SECURA hidden substance: Forms View - MTG = 2,072 cells EXACTLY

The predicted live case is confirmed to the cell: "Forms View - MTG" carries
2,072 non-empty cells with hidden:true, plus three more hidden sheets for a
2,234-cell hidden total. Censused fully; extraction policy deliberately
unchanged here (CE3 flips it).

### F-C4. The predicted 148-col Client Master PCM DOES NOT EXIST in this corpus

The pack said to expect Client Master PCM at 148 cols, past the 128 embed cap.
OBSERVED: ExcelJS reports 689x71 for that sheet; true extent 70 cols. No sheet
in the whole 10-file corpus exceeds 128 real cols (widest: "Product Component
Model Hacked" at 97, "GL Forms Specifications" at 91). The >128-col wide-matrix
loss (stage0-router warned non-goal) is therefore UNTESTABLE on this corpus -
CE3 needs a synthetic wide fixture or the missing reference workbooks (memory
says 17 were planned; 6 are present).

### F-C5. SECURA Ref Connect Pull: stacked regions across big blank runs work

4,983 x 41 sheet segments into 3 regions across blank runs (the pack warned of
gaps up to 16 rows). Gap-run >= 2 splitting plus disjoint-band splitting held.

### F-C6. Census cost on the biggest file: 1.3 GB child RSS

All_Lines_Master.xlsm (129,642 cells) censuses in ~10s but at 1,277 MB child
RSS - the formatting walk doubles ExcelJS's own footprint. Fine inside this
CLI's isolated child process; NOT yet safe to wire into the live import path.
CE3 should add a formatting-lite census mode before any live wiring.
